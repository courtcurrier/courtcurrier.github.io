My dissertation was all biogeochemistry and statistics. Then Mark Klett's Phoenix Transect pushed me to go back to my field site in the Chihuahuan Desert and look again. Ecologists spend a lot of time outside, but our personal stories from the field, what draws us out there, that storm we escaped or near-miss encounter with a rattlesnake rarely get the stage. This is a place for those stories. These days I collect them by bike. Natural history at the speed of a bicycle: slow enough to stop for a lizard or a cool plant, fast enough to cross a whole desert valley in a day. Welcome to the Saddle Transect!
