how do grassy ecosystems adapt to a changing world?

Research Publications Fieldwork Paintings About

Deserts, grasslands, and savannas cover nearly half of Earth’s land surface and account for one-third of global carbon fixation. Semi-arid regions alone drive over one-third of variability in the global carbon cycle, owing to their vast extent and sensitivity to global change. Understanding these systems and their responses to global change is imperative for the integrity of Earth and its inhabitants, including humans.
